[ 01 ] // ABOUT

About Me

A focused, honest overview of where I am today and where I am heading.

I am Joseph Abraham, a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) graduate based in Kerala, India. My interest in cybersecurity started with a simple question — how do defenders actually spot an attack before it causes damage? That question led me into security operations, log analysis, and ethical hacking.

I am currently studying for the Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) course, where I am learning reconnaissance, scanning and enumeration, vulnerability assessment, web application security fundamentals, and authorized penetration testing methodology. Alongside the course, I practise in hands-on environments such as TryHackMe and Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges, documenting what I learn as I go.

My main area of interest is Security Operations Center (SOC) workflow — how alerts are triaged, how logs from Windows and Linux systems are read and correlated, how indicators of compromise are identified, and how incidents are investigated and reported. I am building foundational knowledge in SIEM concepts, the incident response lifecycle, phishing analysis, network traffic investigation, the MITRE ATT&CK framework, and vulnerability management.

I practise ethical, authorized security testing only — inside labs, CTF platforms, and systems I own or have explicit permission to test. I do not test systems without authorization.

My goal is to secure an entry-level SOC Analyst or Cybersecurity Analyst position where I can contribute to real security operations, learn from experienced analysts, and keep improving every single day.
